# 📊 Power BI Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. It transforms financial sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand business performance and identify important trends.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales, profit, product performance, geographical distribution, and discount analysis through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Compare product-wise sales and profitability.
- Explore country-wise sales performance.
- Analyze sales distribution across business segments.
- Understand the impact of different discount bands.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Analysis
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Product-wise sales comparison.
- Country-wise sales visualization.
- Sales distribution by business segment.

### 2. Profit Analysis
- Product-wise profit comparison.
- Monthly profit trend analysis.
- Year-wise profit analysis.

### 3. Interactive Filters
Users can dynamically filter the dashboard based on:
- Year
- Country
- Segment
- Product

### 4. Additional Insights
- Discount band analysis.
- Gross sales comparison.
- Units sold by country.
- Geographical sales distribution.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation.
- **Data Visualization** – Presenting business insights through interactive charts.
